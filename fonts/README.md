# Fonts that come with Folio

These fonts are free to use, including on books and products you sell,
and may be shipped with software. They come from Google Fonts.

Each folder holds one font family and its licence:

- `OFL.txt`: SIL Open Font License 1.1
- `LICENSE.txt`: Apache License 2.0

The fonts may not be sold on their own. `fonts.json` lists every family
and its group; `tools/fetch_fonts.py` downloads the set again.
